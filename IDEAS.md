# Ideas for later

Parked Oct 26, 2026. Offseason emails, to discuss before building. Today the Tuesday email only covers game weeks, so nothing goes out from February to September.

## After the draft (late April)

- One email per subscriber's schools: who was drafted, round and pick, and the NFL team.
- Undrafted free agent signings from the same schools in the days after.
- Open question: send the Monday after the draft, or wait a week so UDFA signings are in.

## Trades, free agency and cuts

- Players from your schools who changed teams: free agency in March, trades through the deadline, roster cuts in late August.
- Open question: a digest at each of those three points, or folded into one preseason email.

## Preseason (August into September)

- Who made the 53-man roster after cuts, who landed on practice squads, and who starts Week 1 per the depth charts.
- Could double as the "season is back" email that restarts the Tuesday cadence.

## Things to check before building

- Whether nflverse roster data updates fast enough for a draft or cuts email (draft picks show up in the draft file quickly; rosters lag).
- Resend's free tier is 100 emails a day, so any one-time blast has to fit that or be split across days.
