# Surface: CFB Sundays app (site/index.html)

Mode: Operate. A working tool used on phones during Sunday games and on desktop midweek. Audience: die-hard college fans plus friends they share it with. Job: pick schools, see where former players are playing and how they did, compare schools. Constraints: every tab and feature stays; fast, ad-free; follows light/dark; plain copy voice; team identity by color only, no school, NFL or video game logos or wordmarks.

## Direction contract

THESIS: CFB Sundays as an early-2000s console college football menu: the site is your team-select screen. It refuses the neutral sports-app shell (white cards, grey tabs, one accent).

OWN-WORLD: Glossy beveled menu bands in team color over a steel ground (light) or deep stadium-navy ground (dark); slanted parallelogram tabs and chips; heavy condensed italic display set in Saira at narrow width, body Saira at normal width; tabular numerals; a gold selection bar as the one bright highlight; chrome hairline highlights on band edges. No neon glow, no logos.

STORY: A visitor picks schools, sees the shell take on their lead school's colors, scans this week's games as score bugs, opens a game, and digs into rankings.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top: a team-color header band with the wordmark "CFB SUNDAYS" set slanted, week and season at right. Below: school chips as slanted tags in each school's colors, then the search box. Then the tab rail with the gold selection bar on the active tab. Then the week selector and the first game score bugs. Primary action: picking a school (search) sits directly under the band.

Signature move: the team-select reskin (header band and selection colors take on the lead school's colors with a slanted wipe) plus the gold selection bar that slides between tabs.
